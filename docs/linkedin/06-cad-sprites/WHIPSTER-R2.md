# Whipster — CAD sprite sheet, round 2: PASS

Checked: re-rendered s92-side / s92-front stills at full size + the final sheet.

## What changed

- **The rotor is at the top.** The blade line spans the full width above the fuselage
  in the side view and above the mast in the front view. The helicopter reads as a
  helicopter now.
- **Windows read as windows.** Smaller, with gaps; the cockpit glass is a nose window
  with mullions, not a black patch.
- **Wheels are outline-only.** The octagon circles + hub discs read as wheels; the
  black blobs are gone (both assets).
- **Fin matches the fuselage tone.** The tail no longer looks bolted on.

## Nits (recorded, not rebuild triggers)

- The tail rotor still reads as a small diamond smudge on the fin in the side view —
  it sits where a tail rotor belongs, but the exact geometry is muddy at this scale.
- The cabin windows are uniform dark squares (smaller now, acceptable); the glass /
  glass_dim variation is sub-visible on paper.
- The front view's fuselage circle + windshield still faintly suggests a face at a
  glance; the mast, engines, sponsons and gear anchor it as a helicopter.
- Wheel octagons (HLR discretisation of the cylinders) are visible at full size.
- The bus top view is a rectangle with a roof pod — honest (a bus from above IS that),
  but the emptiest cell on the sheet.

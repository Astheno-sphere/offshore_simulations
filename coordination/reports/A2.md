## searoute vs SeaGraph (the number)
Land: Natural Earth 1:110m (shipped inside pyogrio's test fixtures; very coarse, so SeaGraph is optimistic in absolute terms). SeaGraph spacing and clearance 2.5 km, endpoints in open water (snap 0 m). Neither side tuned.

| Pair | great circle | SeaGraph | searoute incl. stubs | searoute lane only | stubs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| (4.5E 59.0N) to (9.5E 57.8N), round Lindesnes | 320.3 km | 331.4 km | 445.6 km (+34.4 %) | 393.9 km (+18.8 %) | 51.7 km |
| (3.8E 57.5N) to (4.2E 60.0N), open North Sea | 278.9 km | 279.6 km | 344.5 km (+23.2 %) | 254.8 km (-8.9 %) | 89.7 km |

Finding, not a bug: searoute is 23 to 34 % longer than SeaGraph once it is made to start and end at our points, because its ~20 km lane mesh has no node near open-water points and it ignores land for the stub (the lane-only figure is even shorter than the great circle in the open case, which is the nearest-node snap). Two pairs is a smoke test, not a calibration. Do not use searoute distances in a solver matrix for Norwegian coastal legs; SeaGraph stays the router.

## Gate 4b: what a real run compares
Decoded track vs simulated: sailing speed p10/p50/p90 (kn) and per-place port-call dwell p10/p50/p90 (s), over a caller-set window that contains at least one full call per place. Pass if `|sim - ais| <= max(25 % of ais, 600 s or 1.0 kn)`. **The tolerances are `assumption: true`** (the pre-existing `compare_to_sim` defaults; nothing sources them). Implemented and unit-tested: `compare_decoded` passes a matching sim, fails a 6 kn sim and a 30 min dwell sim, leaves a window without a call as NaN (not judged), and refuses an empty window.

## Could not prove offline / open risks
- Real NMEA: the live feed's tag blocks, `\c:` timestamps, `!BSVDM` talkers, class B (types 18/19/24) and type 27 are not covered; type 18 is rejected as unsupported, not decoded. That is the biggest gap for PSV traffic that includes small craft.
- pyais does not verify checksums; I verify them in `decode_nmea`. Fixture sentences were built with pyais's own encoder, so decode-of-encode is a round trip, not independent evidence of pyais correctness.
- `contract.validate` reads a row's state as its outgoing segment, but `compare_to_sim` reads `state[1:]` (the arriving segment) for moving-ness. A simulated dwell row followed by a zero-distance `sail` row counts as a 0 kn "moving" segment in the sim speed quantiles. Pre-existing, not changed (it is in the pipeline's gate). `to_trajectory` follows `validate`.
- Only two searoute pairs, on coarse land. A real comparison needs the Kartverket coastline world and 10 to 20 PSV pairs (fetch-world).
- Tests changed: none edited. New tests only.

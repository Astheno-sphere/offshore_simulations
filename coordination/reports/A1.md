## Finding that shaped the design: ortools and highspy cannot share an interpreter
Both wheels ship HiGHS symbols. `import ortools.constraint_solver.pywrapcp` after `highspy` fails with `undefined symbol`
(setLocalOption...), and `highspy` after ortools fails with `undefined symbol Highs::releaseMemory`. pyomo (`appsi_highs`) and
whelp use highspy, and gate 1b runs pyomo after the chosen solver. So the model runs in a subprocess; the parent never imports
ortools (`test_ortools_and_highspy_share_one_process`). Cost: ~0.5 s process start per solve. Versions that clash: ortools 9.15.6755 with highspy 1.15.1.

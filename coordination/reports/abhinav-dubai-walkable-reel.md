# Study: abhinavbwj reel "How to make Dubai walkable with Grasshopper?" (Instagram DgHs3S9oH-d, posted 2026-11-24)

Method: viewed in the owner's Chrome (Claude in Chrome), muted, 5 zoomed frames at roughly 0, 4, 8, 14 and 20 s after load plus the page text.
No download, no frames committed (reference rule). Limits: about 5 samples, no cut times, no audio, loop point unknown, exact run time unknown.
Numbers below are observed layout fractions, not measured by `eyes` (no file). Redo with `eyes dna` when a screen recording of the reel is saved.

## What it is
- Vertical 9:16 split screen. Top half (about 48 %): the live Grasshopper canvas, grey, node wires, panels. Bottom half (about 52 %): the Rhino perspective viewport. The tool is the picture.
- The viewport camera does not move in any sampled frame. Every change comes from layers switching on the same locked view.
- Layer sequence seen: street and footprint polylines in red on mid grey; "every building" extruded to white massing; selected buildings pink with red edges; a park in flat green; final pass colours every street metre red or blue (sun or shade, the comments say Ladybug).
- Data source is OSM inside Grasshopper (an OSM import component, a metadata lookup for the height tag, then extrusion). Same recipe as our `osm.py` heights, and its data trees are on screen (flatten tree etc.).

## Caption and number craft
- One yellow label chip at the seam between the two halves, centred, same position every beat. Shaped like a Grasshopper panel: yellow fill, thin dark border, small grip dots at both ends.
- Text is a short clause, 3 to 6 words, lowercase sentence fragments that run on from chip to chip ("then every building", "so every sunny metre"), read as one spoken sentence.
- Numbers are the largest type on screen, bold, unit small beside it, a tiny qualifier line below ("min / to walk 184 m"). Forms seen: a before-to-after pair with an arrow (25 to 7 min), a single time with a distance, a ratio.
- The result chip (25 to 7 min between two towers) is on screen at the first frame I captured: the payoff may be the thumbnail. Unverified until the loop point is known.

## Palette and light (correcting an assumption)
- This reel is not oxblood. It is mid grey ground, white massing, red network lines, pink selection, flat green park, blue and red analysis, yellow chips. No gradients, no glow, no cast shadows; soft flat shading only.
- Saturated colour carries data; the ground stays neutral. One new colour idea per beat.
- Our owner palette (oxblood, gold, orange, charcoal; Montserrat) is the owner's choice for our brand, not something this reel evidences.

## Density and scale
- Real city blocks at density: hundreds of footprints and a skyline of towers in one viewport, streets as continuous polylines. Nothing is a sprite or an icon.
- Annotations sit on the objects (route lines, chips), not in a dashboard column. Only one text element at a time.

## Engagement signals (page text, 1 day)
- 27 comments, 35 reposts. Praise names the method and the process shown, and "intelligent use" of the analysis beyond automation; viewers ask for the full tutorial and whether it works for any city.

# V1 report: the visual gate (2026-11-12)

Branch `agent/claude/V1-visual-gate`. Files: `offsim/cinema/visual_gate.py` (new), `tests/test_visual_gate.py` (new),
`tests/golden/` (new: 3 PNG + 3 `.text.json` + `make_goldens.py`), `offsim/motion/findings.py` (skia port),

## What it does
`inspect(img, spec)` measures a frame, `passes(report, strict)` returns `(ok, ["<rule>: <measured vs limit>"])`,
`compare(frame, golden, tol)` flags a structural change. HARD rules always fail; SOFT rules fail only with `strict: true` and
are otherwise `report["warnings"]` (printed by `story` dry-run, returned through `check(..., warnings=[])`).
`capture_text()` records every `Canvas.text` and PIL `ImageDraw.text` call so contrast, margins and type scale use the film's own
text boxes. `story.check` renders the dry-run frame of every `section` and `findings` shot (1080 x 1350 takes 0.4 to 0.6 s to
inspect; a section frame adds ~1.5 s of render) and refuses with `shot N (type): visual gate: <rule>: ...`.

## Every threshold and its source (all in `GATE_SPEC`, each group has a `src`)
| Rule | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| edges | Sobel step >= 3 L* is an edge; band overall 0.050 to 0.374, main panel (y 17 to 75 %) 0.043 to 0.374 | goldens: findings 0.0997 / 0.0870 (lowest), map 0.1871 (highest); 0.5 x and 2 x |
| strokes | median equivalent width >= 0.75 px at the delivered size; strong ink = >= 25 % of ink-vs-ground contrast; 11 px grey-opening surround | CAD rule 4 (0.75 px is dropped); goldens 0.90 to 1.45 px at 1080. Brief said 1 px: the findings frames measure 0.97, so 1 px fails the references on muted type. Deviation, flagged |
| palette | off-palette share <= 5 %; tolerance 4.9 Delta E (0.5 x median NN distance of 30 declared colours, floor 3); gamut = declared colours + every two-colour blend | brief V1; goldens 0.5 to 0.8 % |
| accent | hard > 8 %; soft < 0.1 % | rule 6 and brief V1; the brief's 1 % floor holds for no reference (0.2 to 0.4 %), so the floor is half the lowest golden |
| contrast | WCAG ratio >= 4.5 on a flat ring around each text box (hard); textured ring (robust L* spread > 3) is soft | WCAG 2.x AA; the texture split is a definition, measured on the section golden |
| type_scale | soft, > 4 distinct sizes excluding the methods line | rule 17 (3) and brief V1 (4) |
| margins | safe 3 % of the short side; band ink <= 0.4 %; clip = ink in the outer pixel row/column; bleed boxes (section sea panel y 17 to 43 %) exempt for ink only | findings golden >= 3.2 % from the edge, band ink 0.0 %; EBU R95 3.5 % noted |
| balance | hard: smallest half of the ink >= 13 %; soft: <= 3 of 9 cells under 20 % of mean density | goldens: smallest half 0.27 (findings), 0.42 (section); sparse cells 0, 2, 0 |
| compare | 4 x 4 regions, tolerance 35 % of max(golden, floor 0.04 ink / 0.008 edge), 1 px blur first | noise sigma 3 and a 1 px shift stay inside; a removed headline band fails |

The goldens are our own frames (judged good by eye in v7 to v10), one per kind: the bands are as wide as three references allow.

## What it catches (each has a synthetic test that breaks exactly one rule)
empty frame, mush, off-palette slab, accent flood, low-contrast text (from text metadata), ink clipped at an edge, text outside the
safe area, an empty half, hairline strokes, a six-size type scale (strict), a headline that runs off the frame (via `story.check`).
It also found real defects while porting: the one-vessel `state_gantt` was a 60 % void (now tall lanes plus a "time in each state"
block from `totals_h`), and the old findings text used 7 sizes (now 13 / 15 / 22 / 52).

## Verdict on the current demo section frame (`tools/section_film.gale_shot`, 1080 x 1350, frame 0 after 40 spin-up steps)
World data is absent here, so the demo renders without its map panel: hard PASS, strict FAIL (warnings: 2 muted text boxes over the
streak field at 2.95:1 and 4.03:1; 8 text sizes where rule 17 allows 3 plus the methods line). With a synthetic coast standing in

1350 (1.5 % from the bottom edge, limit 3 %). This is the committed section golden's known verdict (a test pins it).

## What it cannot judge
Whether the physics, numbers or labels are right; wrong geometry (a rotor at ground level passes); taste; colour-blind legibility;
motion and timing; map, card and equation shots (they render outside Python, no frame here); text drawn by other libraries.

## Open risks
1. **section.py will be refused once the world data is present.** Footer at 98.5 % height (hard) plus 8 type sizes and low
   contrast over streaks (soft). Fix in section.py (move the footer up ~25 px, one type scale, lift muted text over the field), or
   waive per shot with `visual: {spec: {margins: {safe: 0.01}}}` knowingly. `test_section_golden_verdict_is_the_known_footer_finding`
   fails when it is fixed: regenerate the golden and assert a pass. I did not touch section.py (not mine).
2. `check()` gates one frame per shot (the dry-run frame, indicative deck, early plume). A late-frame defect is invisible; gate first,
   middle and last for the final film.
3. Thresholds rest on three references. Add references (any frame the owner accepts) to `make_goldens.py` and re-run `--table`.
4. Strokes: 0.75 px not the brief's 1 px (see table). Equivalent width mixes width and contrast by design.
5. `capture_text` patches `Canvas.text` and `ImageDraw.text` at class level while active: not thread-safe. PIL text boxes are
   approximated (top 0.12 em to 1.1 em).
6. Golden PNGs are 41 KB (findings, map) and 108 KB (section; the streak texture does not compress). They depend on the pinned
   skia 144.1 and bundled Inter; `test_goldens_match_what_the_renderers_produce_today` compares structurally, so small raster
   differences pass.
7. Test counts: origin/main at b88709b collects 612 tests in this workspace (not 670); with V1 it is 642, full run 635 passed,
   7 skipped, 0 failed (none of the skips are in V1's files). `tests/fixtures/intake/synthetic_paper.pdf` restored after the run.
8. The `map` golden is the panel alone, so its balance and bleed settings are a `kind: map` preset; a map shot is not gated in
   `check()` (deck.gl renders it).

# V1: the intervention shot kind

Worktree: /tmp/v1i, branch agent/claude/V1-intervention (from origin/main). /tmp/v1 was occupied by another agent's V1-visual-gate worktree.

## Built
- `offsim/physics/panel2d.py`: MAC-grid 2D solver. Semi-Lagrangian RK2 plus MacCormack (clamped), direct sparse pressure solve (rebuilt on geometry change), no-slip, power-law boundary-layer inflow times seeded perturbation, outflow p=0, lid top. `Subject`: solid, momentum source, heat, porous drag. `schedule(t, None)` switches the subject while the solver keeps stepping.
- `offsim/physics/masks.py` (polygon and stroke rasterisation, pocket fill), `FieldStreaks` appended to `offsim/physics/streaks.py` (full-panel, length proportional to speed, hole-first reseeding, never into solids, static kill, `measure()` gives cover and static share).
- `offsim/motion/intervention.py`: the shot. Two cases, one clock, acts and event dim from `rules.INTERVENTION`, chips, CAD hidden-line world (QA-passed platform_jacket, s92, person), obstacle mask = orthographic shadow of the CAD solids at 9 px/m, 3 persons per panel, amber subject as the only saturated element, caption block, forcing from `report:weather.*` refs (literal forcing refused), DRAFT in the kicker for synthetic or assumed inputs. CLI renders one still per act.
- `offsim/cinema/visual_gate.py`: frame-budget rules scene_height, caption_height, text_roles, forbidden, saturated_hues, scale_figures, field_cover, field_static, all HARD, numbers read from `rules.FRAME_BUDGET`. `story.py`: registration, check, dry-run quick frame.
- Demo: `offsim/papers/irinagribkovskaia-2025-tactical-helicopter/storyboard.intervention.yaml` (S-92 on the Floro helideck, downwash as momentum source, fuselage solid, platform in section, flight leaves; cases = run weather q10 vs max wind).
- Tests: `tests/test_intervention.py` (28). Goldens: `tests/golden/intervention_{before,event,after}.*` (360x450 plus text and meta sidecars), via `make_goldens.py --intervention` (not in `all_frames()`).

## Physical test margins (tests/test_intervention.py, 100x50 grid, dx 0.5, U 4 m/s)
- Mass: max|div u| 8e-15 (limit 1e-8); inflow 93.2096 = outflow 93.2096 (limit 1e-10 relative).
- Velocity exactly 0 in solid cells and closed faces.
- Wake: mean speed differs by 0.26 U subject on vs off (limit 0.15 U).
- Switch-off: viscous steady wake (nu=1, no noise) RMS distance 0.52 -> 0.06 m/s after 12 s (limit: a third). Plain statement: at the film's viscosity (nu 0.03 plus Smagorinsky) the wake sheds and decorrelates, so my first test (time-mean convergence of the shedding wake) FAILED and I rewrote it to the steady regime. Why: pointwise convergence is not a property of a chaotic wake. Convergence in the film is statistical only.
- Determinism per seed, differing between seeds; heat plume rises; porous region drags; streaks never in solids, cover and static within budget.

## Does the field reorganise (film run, measured)
Case 1.5 m/s: reorg_share 0.46, deck speed change 27 %, deck turbulence 0.048 -> 0.184. Case 5.7 m/s: reorg_share 0.70, deck speed change 14 %, lee change 0.50. Measurable. Visually, on the dim-lit, mostly-eddy field, the change at the stills is subtle: the S-92's downwash is a small local disturbance inside a large vortical lee that exists with or without it. No physics failure, but the cause-effect is not obvious to the eye (see below).

## Where the stills fall short
- Cause is weak to the eye. The lee eddies dominate; the rotor wash is tiny. A viewer sees two very similar fields. Honest fix needs a stronger or larger subject, a calmer base flow, or a difference-field cue (not allowed: no colour bar).
- The reference grammar's panels differ strongly between cases; ours differ mainly in speed texture, since synthetic weather is mild (1.5 vs 5.7 m/s) and from one direction.
- 2D extrusion: a rotor disc is a slice, no 3D vortex ring. Lid blockage inflates the lee. Jacket, crane and flare are open lattice (a thin solid wall would be wrong either way).
- Event-act streaks are widened by 1/dim so the dim does not push the stroke test under 0.75 px; that is a drawing decision to keep tails legible, flagged here.
- Edges band: an intervention-specific band is added in KINDS["intervention"] as an ASSUMPTION; no reference measurement backs it.
- from_right wind is not built. Thrust fraction 0.1, MTOW 12020 kg and wind direction are assumptions (marked in the storyboard; they force DRAFT). No video, no ffmpeg.

## Per-frame cost (CPU, this workspace)
Grid 300x139 at dx 0.4 m, 5500 streaks per panel, tail 24 frames, 2 solver steps per frame. Sim 0.21-0.24 s per frame for both panels, draw about 1.2 s per frame. Master-field flush plus per-case spin-up about 70 s one-off. Three stills about 4.5 min. 600 frames (20 s at 30 fps) about 15 min plus spin-up, so use the `render` workflow for the film.

## Three things about the form I think are wrong

1. **The cases rhyme too closely.** Calm (1.5 m/s) and windy (5.7 m/s) panels read as
   near-duplicates at a glance — same platform, same field character. The numbers differ
   (u10, reorg, deck speeds) but the eye can't tell the cases apart without reading the
   chips. The form demands "two cases that differ visibly"; synthetic mild winds don't
   deliver. Real winters will separate them — the form should refuse a pair whose
   measured case separation is this small, not just count to two.
2. **The effect is measured but not legible.** Reorg 0.46–0.70 says half to two-thirds of
   the field changed, yet before vs after look near-identical without side-by-side study:
   the downwash hides inside the platform's own lee vortex, which exists with or without
   the helicopter. The form's core promise (§1: "let physics show what it was doing") is
   only half-met — physics shows *a* change, but the eye can't name it. A film needs the
   switch to dominate the field, not hide inside it. The gate should measure legibility
   (e.g. the reorg concentrated where the subject was), not just total reorg.
3. **Scale figures pass the gate but fail the eye.** The gate counts 3 per panel; at still
   size they are invisible — I could not find them in any of the three frames. "True
   scale" is then a number the eye can't check. The form needs a legibility floor for
   scale figures (a minimum rendered size), not just a count.

The headline problem is a script problem (§6), not a form problem, and is recorded
above rather than counted here.

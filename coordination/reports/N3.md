## What changed, per asset

### aw189 — the solid was correct; the measurement was wrong (plus one real solid error the check exposed)
- Verified the true rotor diameter as 2x the max tessellated tip radius from the hub axis: **14.604 m** vs declared
  14.60 m (**0.027%**) — the solid is correct. Proof the bbox can never span it: 5 blades at 72 deg, no two oppose,
  so the widest bbox axis is 2R·cos(18 deg) = 13.89 m + chord = 13.99 m measured. Fix is a correct measurement:
  `qa.py` gains axis `"r"` (radial: 2x farthest tessellated vertex from `meta["pivots"][part]`), registry aw189
  `rotor_diameter_m` now `{axis: r, part: "=rotor"}`. The same radial check already exists in
  `tests/test_cad_marine_air.py::_max_radius` — the gate now measures what the tests already proved.
- Fuselage / height verified against the Leonardo data sheet and the EASA OEB dimensioned drawing: overall length
  17.57 m, overall height 5.07 m (Leonardo current Energy brochure 2025; EASA drawing says 17.57 / 5.06 m),
  tail-rotor Ø 2.90 m — all match the solid. **Real solid error found and fixed: rotor-head height was 4.45 m
  (assumption); the EASA OEB §3.2 drawing gives 4040 mm — `hub_top` corrected 4.45 → 4.04, now sourced.**
  `tail_rotor_diameter_m` also sourced from the same drawing (Ø2900 mm).
- Still assumed, with reason: `fuselage_length_m` 15.40 m (no public figure — the EASA §3.1 table's "17.605 m" is the
  overall length, and §3.2 gives no airframe length); `width_m` 3.00 m (drawing's front view gives 2.55 / 2.80 /
  3.02 m — fuselage / sponsons / max — against the model's 3.00 m fuselage+sponsons).
- Sources: Leonardo AW189 https://www.leonardo.com/en/-/aw189-1 (via fairlifts spec page); EASA OEB draft report
  14/01/2014 §3.1/§3.2 https://www.easa.europa.eu/sites/default/files/dfu/certification-experts-docs-oeb-reports-agusta-AW189-Draft-OEB-Report-10-01-14.pdf

### platform_jacket — re-parameterised to a real, published platform (Solan)
- Defaults are now the Solan platform (Premier Oil Decommissioning Programmes AB-SO-LAP-LL-PM-PG-0001 Rev B01,
  May 2024, §1.3/§2.1): 4-legged steel jacket, water depth ~136 m (LAT), jacket 158.5 m high, 20x20 m at the top,
  45x45 m at the base.
  https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/669a21af0808eaf43b50d290/Solan_Decommissiong_Programmes.pdf
- `water_depth_m` 136.0, `deck_elev_m` 22.5 (= 158.5 − 136, derived), `top_spacing_m` 20.0, batter solved from the
  published top/base/height so the outside base width is exactly 45.0 m; 4-leg plan made square (was 26 x 20.8 m).
  Foot cans reduced to leg OD (were 1.25x) — the flare pushed the measured base width past the published 45.0 m.
- Registry `measure:` entries: `height_m` {z, jacket} (whole-shape z is the crane-boom tip, 197.8 m),
  `base_width_m` {y, jacket}, `deck_width_m` {y, decks}. Dims are computed from the parameters, sourced only when
  the parameters reproduce Solan; other parameter sets give assumptions (same pattern as the PSV).
- Measured height is 158.794 m, not 158.5 m: the +0.294 m is the top frame tube radius above the 158.5 m leg-top
  node — inside the 1% gate, noted in the registry.
- Still assumed, with reason: `deck_width_m` 26.0 m (3 m overhang past the 20 m top; the Solan document gives no
  topsides plan dimensions) and all topsides/leg-OD/bay geometry (generic North Sea practice).

### tree — default specimen moved inside its cited range + the missing dim added
- `crown_diameter_m` added (same physical quantity as `crown_width_m`, under the registry's required-dim name).
- Norway spruce default height **25 → 30 m**: 25 m sat below the cited mature range (30–46 m), so it could not be

  spruce defaults are assumptions encoded the out-of-range 25 m default; it now asserts the spruce defaults are

## Comparison with the grammar (VISUAL_GRAMMAR.md)

Agreements: one counterfactual, shown not told; 0 cuts; static camera;
subject the only saturated element; field fills the panel and never stops;
after is the longest act (45%); no chart, no colour bar, no footnote.

Differences — the evolution (same list in N8/N9; this is the form's
second, third and fourth data point, and the grammar was written from
film 01 alone):

1. **Panels are views, not cases.** Grammar §2 says "Cases: 2, same
   treatment, stacked". Films 02, 03, 04 all have ONE case and TWO VIEWS
   (section + map). Film 01 was the exception. Amendment: two panels are
   either two cases of one question, or section + map of one case.
2. **Act shares: the after is always 45%; the event is as long as the
   striking takes.** Ténéré's event is 1 s (a truck); Prometheus 2 s (a
   cut); Tree of Knowledge 3 s (a poisoning). Grammar "40/15/45" → "after
   45%, event as long as the striking, before the rest". This resolves
   the N6 difference (2): the event starts when the subject is struck.
3. **Text roles: the honest count is 7, and the films carry a small
   legend + a micro-readout inside the scene.** Kicker and case chip are
   the same text shown twice (one role); plus state chip, map chip,
   headline, body, handle = 7. The section's top-right legend (5 items)
   and the map's micro-readout are part of the form, small and
   corner-placed. FRAME_BUDGET's cap of 5 and its "no readout" rule
   would refuse all three films. Proposed: cap 7; "no chart, no colour
   bar, no footnote paragraph; one corner legend (≤5 items) and one
   micro-readout (≤4 numbers) permitted". Arshad + Fable to decide —
   V1's gate enforces the current budget.
4. **Headline ≤7 words is violated.** Ténéré's is 9 words; Prometheus 8;
   Tree of Knowledge 11. Proposed: ≤11 words (measured max).
5. **Scene floor 65% refuses Ténéré (48.9%) and Prometheus (61.8%).**
   Measured range across four films: 49–74%. Proposed: floor 45%
   (lenient, covers all four) or keep 65% and build tighter than the
   reference. Arshad + Fable call.
6. **Scale figures: camels count.** The grammar lists "people, vehicles,
   lit rooms". Ténéré's scale figure is a camel caravan. Proposed:
   "people (or the place's travellers), vehicles where the place has
   them, lit rooms". Vehicles are unconfirmed in all four films — drop
   them or mark them unverified.
7. **Saturated colour: subject only in the section; the map panel is
   coloured by its one scalar** (visibility here; temperature in 04).
8. **Motion peak is not always at the event.** Ténéré peaks at the event
   (15.93, s10); Tree of Knowledge peaks in the after act. The gate rule
   is "displacement > 0 everywhere", not "peak at event".
